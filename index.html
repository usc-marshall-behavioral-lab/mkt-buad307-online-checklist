What to know before, during, and after

Your progress
⚠️ Complete Immediately After Signing Up You cannot save your spot and return later. Holding a spot prevents other students from participating. Incomplete sign-ups are automatically canceled each morning.
1

Before You Start

Environment setup · ~2 min
🔧 Environment
  • Find a quiet spot with no distractions Library, private room — avoid cafés or shared spaces
  • Confirm stable internet connection Wi-Fi or wired — avoid cellular data if possible
  • Use a laptop or desktop computer Not a phone or tablet — some studies don't render correctly on mobile
  • Open Chrome or Firefox browser Safari and Edge may cause compatibility issues
  • Close other browser tabs and apps Prevents slowdowns, accidental tab switches, and pop-ups
  • Silence your phone and minimize distractions
📋 Have Ready
  • Your 5- or 6-digit SONA ID Found in your SONA account profile or confirmation email — you'll enter it during the study
  • Set aside 15–20 uninterrupted minutes Each study is ~10 min, but give yourself buffer time
2

Log In & Find Your Study

Access SONA · ~1 min
🌐 Access SONA
  • Go to marshall-mkt.sona-systems.com
  • Log in with your SONA username and password Username is your USC NetID (e.g., ttrojan)
  • Navigate to Available Studies or click the study link from your invitation email
  • Confirm the study name matches your invitation Each invitation is for a specific study — don't start a different one
  • Click Sign Up (or View/Start if already signed up)
3

During the Study

Complete carefully · ~10 min
💡 Attention Checks Matter Studies include questions to verify you're reading carefully. Failing these may result in no credit.
✅ While Completing
  • Read every question carefully before answering Don't rush — thoughtful responses protect research quality and your credit
  • Watch for attention-check questions e.g., "Select 'Strongly Agree' for this item" — answer exactly as instructed
  • Do not navigate away from the study page Switching tabs or going back can break the survey and lose your progress
  • Enter your 5- or 6-digit SONA ID when prompted This links your responses to your account for credit
  • Complete the entire study in one sitting You cannot pause and return later
4

After Completing

Verify your credit · ~1 min
🔍 Verify Completion
  • Confirm you reached the study's final "Thank You" page If you don't see a confirmation page, your submission may not have recorded
  • Check that you were redirected back to SONA Some studies auto-redirect; others show a completion code to enter manually
  • If given a completion code, enter it in SONA now Don't close the tab before entering — you may lose it
  • Verify the study shows as "Participated" (not "Signed Up") in SONA Go to My Schedule/Credits → check the status column
📧 Missing Credit? Credits typically post within 24 hours. If yours hasn't appeared, email mkt.sona@marshall.usc.edu with your name, SONA ID, and the study name. Include a screenshot of your SONA status if possible.
