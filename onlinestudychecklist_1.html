BUAD 307 · Research Participation

Online Study Checklist

What to know before, during, and after
🔗 SONA 📄 Handout 📋 In-Person
Your progress
⚠️ Read First
  • Finish in one sitting. You can't save your spot. Incomplete sign-ups are canceled each morning.
  • Do the prescreen first. A SONA account alone doesn't unlock studies. That's usually why none appear.
  • Watch the deadline. Credits are due by the date in your handout. No extensions.

🔧 Environment
  • Find a quiet spot and silence your phone A library or private room, not a café
  • Use a stable internet connection Wi-Fi or wired, not cellular
  • Use a laptop or desktop computer Your phone is fine for this checklist. The study itself needs a computer
  • Open Chrome or Firefox browser Safari and Edge can break studies
  • Close other browser tabs and apps Prevents pop-ups and accidental tab switches
📋 Have Ready
  • Your 5-6 digit SONA ID In your SONA profile or confirmation email
  • Set aside 15 uninterrupted minutes Studies take about 10 minutes. Sign up only when you have the time right now

  • Go to marshall-mkt.sona-systems.com Look for "Marketing (MKT)" in the header. BUAD 304 uses a different site
  • Log in with your SONA username and password NetID only, like ttrojan. Not your student ID, and no @usc.edu
  • Navigate to Available Studies or click the study link from your invitation email New studies post most weeks. Spots fill fast, so act quickly
  • Confirm the study name matches your invitation Each invitation is for one specific study
  • Click Sign Up (or View/Start if already signed up)

💡 Watch for Attention Checks Some questions check that you're reading, like "Select 'Strongly Agree' for this item." Answer exactly as instructed. Failing them can mean no credit.
  • Read all instructions carefully before starting
  • Answer every question thoughtfully and honestly Rushed answers can cost you the credit
  • Do not refresh or navigate away from the study page Refreshing or going back can lose your progress
  • Enter your 5-6 digit SONA ID when prompted This is how the credit reaches your account
  • Complete the entire study in one sitting You cannot pause and return later

  • Confirm you reached the study's final "Thank You" page No confirmation page means it may not have recorded
  • Return to SONA, entering a completion code if the study gave you one Some redirect automatically. Enter any code before closing the tab
  • Verify the study shows as "Participated" (not "Signed Up") in SONA Go to My Schedule/Credits → check the status column
  • Don't discuss study content with classmates It skews results for students who haven't taken it yet
📧 Missing Credit? Credits usually post within 24 hours. If yours hasn't, email mkt.sona@marshall.usc.edu with your name, SONA ID, and the study name. A screenshot helps. Quick question? Slack #buad307-research-participation or text (213) 650-0223.
